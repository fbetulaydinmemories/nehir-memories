# 2020 approved scene recordings

Approved by Betül on 2026-10-15 after delivery of six separate previews. The deployed MP3s are byte-for-byte identical to those approved previews; no remixing or further gain reduction was applied. Web Audio fades into unity gain.

The former nine-second procedural WAV approximations have been removed. These replacements use licensed general-purpose recordings, not recordings of Betül, Nehir, their school, or an anime. No voice cloning or new dialogue was generated; no music, film/anime dialogue or identifiable production effects were used. “Evening with Friends: Sofia Coppola” is a field recording of friends discussing films, not a film soundtrack.

All six loops are approximately 56 seconds. Separate scene compositions use irregular event timing and natural gaps. The indoor room/canteen recordings may be shared as beds, but no finished mix is reused. `mix-manifest.json` records each source excerpt, timestamp and processing. `licenses.json` records authors, original pages, licenses and downloaded-source hashes.

## Checks and limits

Every final MP3 was decoded end-to-end. Duration, channel count, sample rate, loudness, true peak and SHA-256 checks are in `technical-checks.json`. These are technical checks, not subjective listening verification. The assistant could not directly listen; naturalness, intelligibility, perceptual event clarity and seamlessness were not certified by listening. The user approved the supplied previews.

Speaker-like bandpass filtering was applied to speech excerpts; original phrase timing is retained. Clip and loop edges have short fades. Outdoor sounds use tree wind/birdsong and gravel footsteps. A touchpad click was optional and was omitted. The source candidates that failed download, and a group recording described as concerned speech, were not used.
